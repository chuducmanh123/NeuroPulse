Title: Systematic review of levodopa dose equivalency reporting in Parkinson's disease
Authors: Claire L. Tomlinson, Rebecca Stowe, Smitaa Patel, Caroline Rick, Richard Gray, Carl E. Clarke
Journal: Movement Disorders, Vol. 25, No. 15, 2010, pp. 2649-2653
DOI: 10.1002/mds.23429
Publisher: Movement Disorder Society / Wiley-Liss, Inc.
Year: 2010

Abstract:
Levodopa equivalent dose (LED) calculation formulas allow comparison of antiparkinsonian drug regimens. To establish consensus conversion factors for calculating LED, we systematically reviewed the medical literature.

Table 1. Conversion factors to calculate levodopa equivalent dose (LED):
Levodopa (standard / immediate release): dose (mg) x 1.
Levodopa (controlled release / CR): dose (mg) x 0.75.
Levodopa with entacapone: levodopa dose (mg) x 1.33.
Levodopa with tolcapone: levodopa dose (mg) x 1.50.
Pramipexole: dose (mg) x 100.
Ropinirole: dose (mg) x 20.
Rotigotine transdermal patch: dose (mg/24h) x 30.
Bromocriptine: dose (mg) x 10.
Cabergoline: dose (mg) x 50.
Piribedil: dose (mg) x 1.
Apomorphine (subcutaneous): dose (mg) x 10.
Selegiline (oral): dose (mg) x 10.
Selegiline (sublingual): dose (mg) x 80.
Rasagiline: dose (mg) x 100.
Amantadine: dose (mg) x 1.
Entacapone (co-administered with levodopa): levodopa dose (mg) x 0.33.
Tolcapone (co-administered with levodopa): levodopa dose (mg) x 0.50.

Summary of Formulas:
Total LED is calculated by multiplying each antiparkinsonian medication daily dose by its respective conversion factor and summing the products.
