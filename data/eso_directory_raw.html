ESO publishes three types of documents

  1. Full ESO Guideline Document with evidence-based recommendations and expert consensus statements (for characteristics see table below)
  2. Expedited recommendation in instances where there is at least one published guideline-changing RCT
  3. White Paper Document based on expert consensus statements (modified Delphi process)

GUIDELINE
DOCUMENT

Evidence-based recommendation

  • Uses GRADE methodology
  • Follows ESO Guideline SOP
  • Provides high-quality outcome
  • Offers detailed document (available online)
  • Entails time-consuming process
  • Is published in the ESJ

Expert Consensus Statement (ECS)

  • Selected format if there is insufficient evidence to provide an evidence-based recommendation
  • Presented in the guideline document with the result of a majority vote

EXPEDITED
RECOMMENDATION
  • Conducted in the presence of at least one published guideline-changing RCT
  • Typically answers one main PICO question
  • Based on GRADE except that a formal systematic review is not mandatory
  • Rapidly available on the ESO website
  • Published in the ESJ

WHITE PAPER
  • Expert consensus based on the available evidence
  • Modified Delphi process
  • Rapidly available on the ESO website
  • Published in the ESJ

Open Call for Participation in ESO Guideline Module Working Groups

The ESO Guideline Board is pleased to announce the plan to update the ESO Guideline on antithrombotic treatment for secondary prevention of stroke and other thromboembolic events in patients with stroke or transient ischemic attack

and to develop an ESO White Paper on The “5D” Guide to Telestroke: Define – Describe – Deploy – Deliver – Drive.

As part of these projects, we are introducing a new open call process for selecting members of the Module Working Groups (MWGs). This initiative is designed to expand opportunities for ESO members – both junior and senior – to actively contribute to our guideline projects.

  • Open Call Deadline: 11 August 2025
  • Who Can Apply: All active ESO members (junior and senior)
